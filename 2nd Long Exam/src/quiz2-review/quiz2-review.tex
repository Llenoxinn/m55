  {MATH 55: Elementary Analysis III -- Second Long Examination}
  {\textbf{What this is.} Quiz~2 (October) covered vector functions
   (Slides~5) and directional derivatives and the gradient (Slides~6). It is
   the best available model of the difficulty of the Second Long Exam.
   Chapter~1 reproduces the quiz exactly as it was given. Chapter~2 solves
   every problem in full and adds, for each one, \emph{what it tests}, a
   \emph{shortcut}, the \emph{traps} and \emph{how it could be changed}.
   Chapter~3 condenses the recurring patterns into a one-page strategy sheet.
   Chapters~4--5 give a predicted quiz in the same format plus extra drills,
   now also covering Slides~7 (vector fields, conservative fields, potential
   functions; no line integrals), with complete solutions.\par\medskip
   Every answer has been checked with a computer algebra system.}
